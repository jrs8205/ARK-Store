Version 1.10.0 was withdrawn after Play Protect warnings were reported; their cause is not known. This version has everything from it: the search for apps on GitHub reaches much further, prereleases are offered with Show beta versions on, a download waiting for the go-ahead is kept when the app is closed, scrolling is smoother, and the settings end with ways to support the development. An install the system refuses is not tried again by itself, and the reason is shown.
