Share an app from its details: the link opens the app's page in the store, or a page that tells where to get it. The details now offer up to three earlier versions of an app from GitHub, Codeberg or GitLab: with root the store installs the one you choose, without root it saves the file for you to install. After going back with root, the newest update stays on offer but is not installed by itself. The button to an app's page names the place the page is on.
