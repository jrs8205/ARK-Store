Every app is shown with its own icon. Material surfaces: on Android 13 and later the screen is drawn as brushed metal lit from above, and the setting under Appearance turns it off. Chips narrow the list to one place. The details say where an installed app came from and whether its signing key matches the version offered. A fork's original is listed on its own, another app under the same package name is shown as what it is, and a search puts the apps it names first.
